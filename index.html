// About

A designer who thinks in grids, not guesses.

I'm a creative and detail-oriented graphic designer with a Bachelor's degree in Visual Media from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Mysuru. My work centres on branding, UI design, and social media advertising — built to communicate first, decorate second.

I've spent the last few years moving between agency floors and edit suites: package design for retail clients, motion edits for e-learning platforms, and static ad campaigns that had to earn attention in a single scroll. Every project starts the same way — understand what the client needs to say, then find the most honest way to say it.
